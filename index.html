02Research

Checking the work of a deep-learning model of the whole Earth.

DLESyM couples a neural atmosphere to a neural ocean and simulates the climate far faster than a traditional physics model can. Speed only matters if the output is right, so that's my side of it: I build the pipelines and diagnostics that hold the model's century-long runs up against reanalysis data.

Under construction Sep 2026 – Present

OmniAge

A real-time triage platform for iOS, built around events. A Django REST service normalizes incoming patient data and publishes it to Kafka. A Spring Boot triage engine consumes those events, keeps a priority queue ranked by acuity, and schedules follow-ups from free-text notes parsed with Gemini. A SwiftUI operator dashboard stays in sync and flags high-priority cases the moment they arrive.

It's running on 100+ simulated records so far, and early projections put triage time down about 40%.
